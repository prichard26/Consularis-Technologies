In practice

Three moves teams repeat until the picture holds

If the graph lies, the analysis lies. Consularis.ai is built to close that gap fast, in language everyone understands.

Model the real world

Start from a template or blank canvas. Add processes, steps, decisions, subprocesses, and the metrics you measure. Keep going until operators nod instead of shrug.

Stress-test with Aurelius

Query what you built, adjust in conversation, and let the structure stay visible. Less “lost in the workshop notes,” more explicit topology.

Ship the intelligence

Run a structured analysis pass for metrics and automation angles, then export a Company Process Intelligence Report for leaders who will never live inside the tool.

Agent

Why Aurelius changes the pace

Aurelius sits on top of the graph so you are never editing a diagram alone. You ask, it responds against the current model, you tighten the story. The loop is usually faster than workshops alone because nothing hides in prose.

What you get when the map is honest

Analysis highlights where time and data leak, which automations deserve integration budget, and what to simplify first. That is the handoff from insight to execution.
